About PickInbox

PickInbox publishes practical comparisons of email marketing and creator tools. The aim is narrow: help someone choose the right platform once, instead of migrating twice.

How we work

  • We test with real lists and real flows, not feature checklists copied from vendor pages.
  • We disclose affiliate relationships on every page where they exist.
  • We name what a tool is wrong for, not just what it is good at.
  • We update pages when pricing, tiers or limits change — each page carries a review date.

How affiliate links work here

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you sign up through one, the vendor may pay this site a commission — at no extra cost to you, and with no effect on what you pay. Commission never decides placement: platforms we would not recommend are simply not listed, and a recommendation can be negative.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell placements, “top spots” or sponsored rankings.
  • We do not publish invented prices or limits — if a figure is uncertain, we say so.
  • We do not give legal, tax or financial advice.

Corrections

Platforms change quickly and mistakes happen. If something here is out of date or wrong, it gets fixed and the review date moves. Accuracy matters more to this site than any single commission.
